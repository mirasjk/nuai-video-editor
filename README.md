**🔁 Cuts out retakes.** Started a sentence three times before getting it right? The tool keeps the good one and drops the rest.

**🎙️ Dubs your video in your own voice.** Instead of a robotic narrator, the translated audio is generated using a clone of the speaker's voice, so the result still sounds like the person on screen.

## 🧭 Your First Edit, Step by Step

This is the general flow of a typical session. Nothing here is permanent — you can always undo and try again.

**1. Bring in your video.** Point the application at the video file you recorded. Common formats like MP4 and MOV are the usual choice.

**2. Let it listen.** The application analyses the audio track to find speech, silence, filler words, and repeated takes. For a long recording, this is the part that takes the most time. Go make a cup of tea.

**3. Review what it found.** You will be shown the cuts it suggests — the pauses, the false starts, the frozen frames. Look them over. Keep what you agree with, restore anything you want to keep.

**4. Turn on subtitles if you want them.** Choose the language being spoken and the language you want the captions in. The text is generated and timed to the audio automatically.

**5. Try dubbing if you need it.** Select a target language and let the tool produce a translated voice track that matches the speaker.

**6. Export.** Save the finished video to your computer. Play it back once before you upload it anywhere, just to be sure it looks the way you expect.

## 🎙️ Subtitles and Dubbing, In Plain English

**Subtitles** are the written words that appear at the bottom of the video. Normally a person has to watch the whole thing and type them out with precise timing. Here, the application listens to the audio, writes down what was said, and lines each line up with the moment it was spoken. Translation then converts those lines into another language while keeping the timing intact.

**Dubbing** goes one step further. Instead of reading captions, viewers hear a voice. The application recreates the sound of the original speaker and uses it to say the translated words. The point is that a viewer in another country hears something that still sounds like you, not a stranger.

Both features work best when your recording is clean. A quiet room and a decent microphone go a long way.

## 💡 Tips for Better Results

- **Record in a quiet space.** Background music, traffic, and echo confuse speech detection. Cleaner audio means fewer mistakes.
- **Speak normally.** You do not need to slow down or over-enunciate. Natural speech is what the tool is trained to handle.
- **Do not edit before you import.** Hand-cut your video afterwards, not before. Let the automatic pass do its job on the raw recording first.
- **Review before exporting.** Automatic is not the same as perfect. A two-minute review saves you from uploading something with a cut in the wrong place.
- **Work on shorter files first.** Try a five-minute clip to learn the interface before throwing a two-hour recording at it.
- **Keep your original file.** Always hold on to the untouched recording until you are happy with the export.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

**The download will not start.** Try a different browser, or temporarily pause any ad-blocking extension on the download page. Corporate and school networks sometimes block file downloads as well.

**The app opens but nothing happens when I add a video.** Very large files take time to read. Wait a minute or two. If it still does nothing, try a shorter clip to confirm the application itself is working.

**The cuts feel too aggressive.** Restore the sections you want to keep and adjust how strictly the tool trims silence. Shorter minimum-pause settings leave more breathing room in the final video.

**Subtitles are slightly out of sync.** Check that the correct spoken language was selected. Mismatched language settings are the most common cause of drifting captions.

**The dubbed audio sounds odd.** Short sentences and clean audio dub far better than long, noisy ones. Re-run the dubbing on a quieter section to compare.

**Everything is slow.** Video analysis is heavy work. Close other demanding programs while it runs, and avoid editing other projects at the same time.
